Design and build the landing page for Ledgerline. Ledgerline turns the payment exports of independent physiotherapy clinics (1 to 5 practitioners) into bookkeeping entries automatically, so the clinic owner stops doing the books on Sunday evenings. It reads exports from the practice-management tools clinics already use (Cliniko, Jane, WriteUpp), matches each payment to the right category and VAT treatment, and hands a clean file to the accountant. It is UK based. Price: 29 pounds a month per clinic, 14-day trial, no card needed. It is new: there are no customers, logos, reviews or usage numbers to show yet. I want clinic owners to start a trial. Make it one page, responsive, and write real copy.

About us: clinic owners use it on a laptop in the evening or a phone between patients. We have no logo, colours or fonts yet. It should feel exact, calm and on your side, never corporate fintech. We like the plainness of a well-kept paper ledger and our accountant's tidy year-end pack; we dislike startup pages with rockets. Plain HTML and CSS, WCAG AA, free fonts only. I cannot answer questions right now, so decide what you need to and tell me your assumptions; pick the direction yourself and say why. Put the page in index.html.
