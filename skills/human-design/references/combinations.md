## Domains

| id | domain | keep | question | twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dev-tools | Developer tools | Dark mode as a first-class mode, often the default; high contrast for code; one signal hue for actions and the current item; status colours that mean pass, fail, running. | Near-black with one neon or an electric violet; or white with Tailwind blue. Both are the category's defaults. | Borrow the colours of the work itself: phosphor, a diff's green and red, warning amber, a terminal theme the audience already knows, the hardware's own paint. |
| ai | AI products | A calm reading surface for long generated text; one clear action colour; visible states for thinking, streaming and done. | Violet-to-blue gradients, glow and glass on near-black. Users now read them as 'another AI wrapper'. | Take colour from the domain the AI serves (law, medicine, code, cooking), not from 'AI'; warm paper and ink read as considered. |
| b2b-saas | B2B SaaS and productivity | Neutral, readable surfaces for all-day use; a primary that marks actions; dense tables that need status colours and chart series. | Corporate blue on white grey, the colour of every other tab the user has open. | Pick a primary from the customer's work (a warehouse orange, a ledger green) and give a second colour a real job, such as the current filter or a highlight. |
| fintech | Fintech and banking | Trust and exactness: steady grounds, tabular figures, unmistakable gains and losses, nothing that looks like a game. | Navy and royal blue, or a challenger's purple; one is the old banks, the other every neobank since 2015. | Deep green, brass, oxblood or teal give trust without the house blue; money's own materials (notes, ledgers, brass) are a source. |
| crypto | Crypto and web3 | Dark trading screens, live numbers, strong up and down colours. | Black with neon green or purple and glow: the default the whole category shares. | Keep the dark ground but drop the neon: soft mint, coral or a jewel tone reads as a product, not a casino. |
| insurance | Insurance | Reassurance and plain language; forms that must feel safe; clear progress through claims. | Blue and white with stock-photo families. | Warmth (marigold, apricot, warm white) with a steady deep teal reads as 'we will help', which is what a claim needs. |
